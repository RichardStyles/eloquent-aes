## 2.0.0 - 2026-02-21

- Minimum PHP version raised to 8.2
- Updated `orchestra/testbench` to ^9.0|^10.0
- Updated `phpunit/phpunit` to ^10.5|^11.0
- Fixed `TestCase::tearDown()` to properly call parent method

- Dropped support for PHP 7.x and 8.0/8.1
